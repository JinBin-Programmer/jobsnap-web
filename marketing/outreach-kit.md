# JobSnap Outreach Kit

Everything you need to get your first 3 customers. Work through this top to bottom.

---

## 1. Who to target

Service companies with **3–15 field workers** whose clients demand proof of work:

| Priority | Industry | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 🥇 | Aircond servicing & maintenance | Scheduled visits, clients expect service reports |
| 🥇 | Pest control | Contracts legally require treatment records |
| 🥈 | Commercial cleaning | Building management demands visit proof |
| 🥈 | Property/facility maintenance | Multiple sites, dispersed teams |
| 🥉 | CCTV/alarm/solar installers | Handover documentation for every install |

**How to build your list:** Google Maps → search "aircond service [area]" → open each listing → save company name + WhatsApp/phone number into a spreadsheet. Target: 50 companies. Prioritise ones with active Facebook pages (they respond faster).

## 2. WhatsApp cold outreach scripts

### First message (BM — use this for most SMEs)

> Hi boss, saya nampak company boss buat servis aircond area [AREA]. 👋
>
> Saya developer — saya baru siapkan satu sistem untuk company servis macam boss:
>
> 📸 Worker snap gambar kerja siap terus dalam app (auto ada lokasi GPS + masa)
> 📄 Boss satu klik je dapat laporan PDF cantik untuk hantar kepada client
> ❌ Tak payah lagi korek gambar dalam WhatsApp group
>
> Saya tengah cari **3 company pertama** untuk guna free sebulan (saya setup semua sekali). Boleh saya tunjuk demo 5 minit?

### First message (EN — for companies that seem English-first)

> Hi, I noticed your company does aircond servicing around [AREA]. 👋
>
> I'm a developer and I've just built a system for service companies like yours:
>
> 📸 Workers snap photos of completed work in an app (GPS + time attached automatically)
> 📄 You get a clean PDF report to send your client in one click
> ❌ No more digging through WhatsApp groups for job photos
>
> I'm looking for the **first 3 companies** to use it free for a month (I'll set everything up for you). Can I show you a 5-minute demo?

### Follow-up (send 2–3 days after no reply — send ONCE only)

> Hi boss, follow up sikit je 🙂 Kalau boss pernah kena client complain "mana bukti kerja?" — ini video 90 saat macam mana sistem saya selesaikan masalah tu: [VIDEO LINK]
>
> Kalau tak berminat, no problem, saya tak ganggu lagi. 👍

### When they reply "berminat" / "what is this?"

> Senang cerita macam ni boss — sekarang worker boss hantar gambar kerja dalam WhatsApp group kan? Lepas seminggu nak cari balik gambar untuk client, memang susah.
>
> Dengan JobSnap:
> 1️⃣ Boss create job dalam website (30 saat)
> 2️⃣ Worker buka app, snap gambar, siap — GPS & masa auto masuk
> 3️⃣ Boss tekan satu button, dapat laporan PDF dengan nama company boss, terus boleh hantar kepada client
>
> Bila boss free? Saya boleh tunjuk melalui video call 5-10 minit, atau saya datang office pun boleh.

## 3. Demo call flow (5–10 minutes)

Prep before every demo: run the seed script so the dashboard looks like a real aircond company, have your phone logged in as a worker.

1. **Ask first, demo second (2 min):** "Boss sekarang macam mana track kerja worker? Client pernah minta bukti tak?" — let them describe the pain. They sell themselves.
2. **Show the loop (3 min):** Dashboard task list → create a job assigned to a worker → on your phone: open the job, snap a photo of anything, submit → back on dashboard: photo + GPS appears → create report → open the client link. **This loop is the whole sale.**
3. **The close (1 min):** "Untuk 3 company pertama saya bagi free sebulan, saya setup semua — masukkan worker boss, install app. Lepas sebulan kalau berguna, dari RM30 sebulan je. Kalau tak berguna, stop, takde kontrak. Nak try?"
4. **If yes — book the setup immediately:** "Ok! Bila saya boleh datang / bila kita boleh call untuk setup? Esok boleh?" Never leave a demo without a scheduled next step.

## 4. Objection answers

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Worker saya tak reti IT" | "Kalau dia reti guna WhatsApp, dia reti guna ni. Satu screen je: snap gambar, tekan hantar. Saya train sekali masa setup — 10 minit." |
| "Mahal la" | "Satu job aircond boss charge berapa? RM150? Sistem ni start dari RM30 untuk SEBULAN — kurang dari satu job. Dan bulan pertama free." |
| "WhatsApp group ok je" | "Ok sampai client tanya 'mana bukti servis bulan lepas?' — nak scroll berapa ribu mesej? Dengan ni, semua gambar dah tersusun ikut job, siap GPS." |
| "Saya fikir dulu" | "Boleh boss. Saya hantar video demo, boss tengok bila free. Saya follow up minggu depan boleh?" (then actually follow up) |
| "Ada app lain macam ni" | "Betul, ada — ServiceM8, Jobber, semua dalam USD, RM300-500 sebulan, dan tak ada orang Malaysia support. Saya orang tempatan, boss WhatsApp saya terus." |

## 5. Pilot deal terms (say this consistently)

- **Free for 1 month**, full setup done by you personally (org, workers, app install, first jobs)
- After 1 month: **RM30/month** (Starter, 1GB) or **RM99/month** (Pro, 5GB) depending on how much photo/video they upload — pay by bank transfer against invoice, no billing system needed yet
- In exchange: honest feedback + (if happy) a testimonial + permission to name them on the landing page
- No contract, cancel anytime — **say this out loud, it removes the fear**

## 6. Daily routine (30 min/day, 4–8 weeks)

- Send 5 new first-messages (from your list of 50)
- Send due follow-ups (one per prospect max)
- Log every contact in the spreadsheet: name / date sent / replied? / demo? / result
- Expected funnel: 50 contacted → ~8 replies → ~4 demos → **1–3 pilots** ✅
